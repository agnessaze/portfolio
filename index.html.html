01 · About

Where process meets decision-making.

I got into Industrial Engineering because I like watching how things actually work. Not the theory of it, the reality: why a step exists, why it takes as long as it does, what happens if you take it away. That's still what I chase, whether it's a factory floor, a robot, or a decision someone's about to make on gut feel alone.

Right now that means splitting my time between the assembly line at Remora Teknoloji, where I'm a production intern, and decision-making research back at school, using AHP, TOPSIS, VIKOR, and FAHP to see what happens when you make an intuitive call explicit instead. Different rooms, same instinct: figure out what's really driving the outcome, then make it visible.

Jul–Aug 2026

04 · Research

Formalizing how decisions get made.

Where manufacturing questions turn into modeling questions.

Hybrid AHP–TOPSIS–VIKOR Decision Model for Manager Selection

Manager-selection decisions usually come down to gut feel. I built a hybrid AHP–TOPSIS–VIKOR model to see what happens when you make the criteria explicit instead, and where the three ranking methods agree, and where they quietly don't.

AHPTOPSISVIKORMCDM

Multi-Agent RL for Distributed Job-Shop Scheduling

What if each machine on the shop floor scheduled itself? I explored decentralized job-shop scheduling with multi-agent reinforcement learning, where every machine is an agent learning its own dispatching policy.
